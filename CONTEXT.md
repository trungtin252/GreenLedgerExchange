# Context

This glossary defines the stable business language of GreenLedger Exchange.
It intentionally describes the domain, not implementation choices.

## Language

**Carbon Project** — A defined initiative that aims to avoid, reduce, or remove greenhouse-gas emissions.

_Avoid_ “project record” when referring to the initiative itself.

**Evidence Manifest** — The accountable inventory of evidence submitted for a Carbon Project.

_Avoid_ “file bundle” when its evidentiary meaning matters.

**Assessment** — A structured evaluation of a Carbon Project or its Evidence Manifest against stated criteria.

_Avoid_ “approval” before a decision is actually made.

**Review Case** — The traceable unit of review that groups an Assessment, findings, and decision history.

_Avoid_ “ticket” for a domain review.

**Credit Batch** — A bounded quantity of carbon credits issued from one verified basis.

_Avoid_ “token batch.”

**Instrument** — The tradable representation of a Credit Batch within the exchange.

_Avoid_ “credit” when distinguishing issuance from trading is important.

**Position** — The quantity of an Instrument held by an organization and available subject to reservations.

_Avoid_ “balance” when the available and reserved quantities must be distinguished.

**Order** — An instruction to buy or sell an Instrument under stated terms.

_Avoid_ “trade” until matching has occurred.

**Reservation** — A temporary claim that makes a quantity or value unavailable for another action.

_Avoid_ “hold” without stating what is reserved.

**Trade** — The matched exchange of an Instrument and consideration between counterparties.

_Avoid_ “order” after execution.

**Journal** — The immutable accounting record of balanced postings that explain a financial or quantity movement.

_Avoid_ “transaction log” for accounting truth.

**Retirement** — The irreversible removal of an Instrument from further trading for a stated purpose.

_Avoid_ “burn” in product-facing language.

**Anchor** — An external, time-verifiable reference that attests to the existence of a record or digest.

_Avoid_ “blockchain proof” when the anchoring mechanism is not material.

**GLX-DEMO** — The fictional sandbox designation for GreenLedger Exchange instruments and experiences.

_Avoid_ presenting it as an official registry designation.

**VND-DEMO** — The fictional sandbox denomination used for examples of consideration in GLX.

_Avoid_ presenting it as Vietnamese đồng or a payment instrument.
